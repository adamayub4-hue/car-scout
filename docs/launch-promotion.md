# Mekivo launch promotion pack

## Core message

Mekivo gives UK car buyers and parts shoppers one clear place to begin. Users can compare routes into major marketplaces and see live eBay UK listings without pretending that a visual match guarantees fitment.

Primary call to action: **Search smarter at mekivo.uk**

Secondary proof point: **Enter a UK registration to identify the vehicle before searching for parts.**

## Launch post

Finding a used car or the right replacement part can mean opening tab after tab. Mekivo gives you one simple starting point for searching UK marketplaces, with live eBay listings and clear compatibility reminders.

Try it free: https://mekivo.uk

#Mekivo #UsedCarsUK #CarPartsUK #CarBuying

## Registration-search launch post

Not sure which exact car model you have? Enter the registration in Mekivo's parts finder and it identifies the make, model, year, engine size and fuel type using official vehicle data. Then use those details to start a more specific parts search.

Always confirm the part number and seller fitment before buying.

Try it: https://mekivo.uk

#Mekivo #CarPartsUK #VolkswagenGolf #MercedesBenzUK

## Fifteen-second vertical advert

**0–3 seconds:** Screen recording of several marketplace tabs. On-screen text: “Searching everywhere for one car?”

**3–8 seconds:** Show Mekivo’s make, model, year and price fields. On-screen text: “Start once with Mekivo.”

**8–12 seconds:** Show marketplace links and live eBay results. On-screen text: “Compare routes. View real listings.”

**12–15 seconds:** Mekivo homepage/logo. Voiceover and text: “Search smarter at mekivo.uk.”

## Fifteen-second parts advert

**0–3 seconds:** Hold or show an unrecognised component. On-screen text: “Know what this part is?”

**3–8 seconds:** Show Mekivo’s parts finder and part-number field. On-screen text: “Search by vehicle, category or number.”

**8–12 seconds:** Show live eBay parts listings. On-screen text: “See likely matches.”

**12–15 seconds:** On-screen warning: “Always confirm the part number and fitment.” Then: “mekivo.uk”

## Fifteen-second registration advert

**0–3 seconds:** On-screen text: “Not sure which model you have?” Show a blurred example number plate being entered.

**3–8 seconds:** Show Mekivo returning the make, model, year, engine size and fuel type. On-screen text: “Identify it from the registration.”

**8–12 seconds:** Show the parts categories and live eBay listings. On-screen text: “Start a more specific search.”

**12–15 seconds:** Mekivo logo and text: “Search smarter at mekivo.uk.” Add: “Always confirm seller fitment.”

## Paid-ad starting point

- Start with one car-search video and one parts-search video.
- Use a small test budget and keep targeting broad enough to learn.
- Optimise for visits to `mekivo.uk`, then compare which advert produces actual searches in Vercel Analytics.
- Stop adverts that receive clicks but no meaningful engagement.
- Do not claim guaranteed compatibility, exclusive prices or complete marketplace coverage.

## Brand and safety rules

- Use **Mekivo**, not the former CarScout name.
- Say “search starting point” or “helps narrow the search.”
- Never say that Mekivo guarantees a vehicle, listing or part will be suitable.
- Do not use marketplace logos in paid creative without checking their current brand permissions.
- Use only original, licensed or properly attributed images and music.
