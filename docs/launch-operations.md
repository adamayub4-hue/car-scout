## Owner monitoring routine

- Open `https://mekivo.uk/admin` while signed into the owner account.
- Check open feedback and complaints daily during launch week, then at least weekly.
- Move each report from **Open** to **In progress** and finally **Resolved**.
- Review recent activity and saved-item totals for sudden drops or unexpected spikes.
- Check Vercel logs after any uptime alert or user report.
- Never share owner-dashboard screenshots containing customer email addresses.

## Deferred registration work

Do not register until the owner is ready to pay and has chosen an appropriate registered office/service address. A home address should not be published casually.

- Companies House online incorporation: currently £100.
- Digital confirmation statement: currently £50 per 12-month payment period.
- UK trade mark standard online application: currently £205 for one class and £60 for each additional class.
- Search Companies House and UKIPO again immediately before paying.
- Consider professional advice when choosing the company structure, registered office and trade-mark classes.
