## Publishing sequence

1. Post Creative A first on both channels.
2. Wait at least 24 hours before Creative B.
3. Wait another 24 hours before Creative C.
4. Keep the posting time broadly similar so time of day does not dominate the comparison.
5. Use the matching tagged link in the profile or post destination.

## Decision rules

- Do not pay to boost a concept before it produces at least one completed search organically.
- After 100 campaign visits, stop or rewrite concepts below a 15% search-completion rate.
- Treat 25% or better search completion as promising.
- Treat 10–12% or better marketplace outbound rate as a candidate for a small paid test.
- Cap the first paid test at £30 total. Do not scale until eBay reporting shows attributed clicks and the payout account is active.

## Claims and safety

- Mekivo is a search starting point, not a seller.
- Never claim guaranteed compatibility, complete marketplace coverage or guaranteed savings.
- Do not display real vehicle registrations or personal data in creative.
- Use platform-licensed audio only when posting inside Instagram or TikTok.
